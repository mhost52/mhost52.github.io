ABOUT COMPANY

PT. Leuwijaya Utama Textile was founded since 1987 in Bandung as a downstream manufacturer that produces high quality polyester fabric. We are a leading integrated textile company whose operations cover the entire spectrum of textile manufacturing, including weaving,  dyeing, and finishing.
Along with the growth and rising demand for textile products in the era of international trade, we are proud to be part of the global market community by contributing high-quality textiles to international market.

KNITTED FABRICS

Our knitted fabrics are available in a wide range of weights and constructions, offering versatility for moslem wear & casual wear with a touch of elegance and performance.

Our Profile

ABOUT COMPANY

Leuwijaya Utama Textile was established since 1987 in Cimahi as a downstream manufacturer specializing in high-quality polyester fabric. We are a leading integrated textile company, encompassing the entire spectrum of textile manufacturing, including weaving, dyeing, and finishing. In alignment with the national emphasis on international trade and globalization, we are proud to be part of the global community and provide an overview of our company and products.

KNITTED FABRICS

Our knitted fabrics come in a wide range of weights and constructions, allowing you to apply special treatments to enhance the value of your product, such as anti-bacteria and perfume.

WOVEN FABRICS

We produce 100% Polyester. Our products are ladies items and men suiting items in formal black and plain dyed. Our formal black fabric is highly regarded in the market for its exceptional quality.

Resources

We pay special attention to the company's resources as we realize that the production of high quality textile must stem from the excellent quality of employees involved, raw materials, and production facilities.

Research and Development

The key factor for the future of PT. Leuwijaya Utama Textile is research and development (R&D). It is through this department that we will be ready to meet the challenges of the future, by conducting various experiments and applying new techniques for the creation of innovative, new fancy items.

The principal policies for the R&D department are:

  • Strengthening the basis and exploration of research in accordance with the company's long-term vision
  • Reinforcing and widening the development of production technology
  • Identifying and realizing products with higher value and quality, based on customer requests

The R&D covers the following aspects:

  • Fabric pattern construction design
  • Application of technology to create special effects on finished goods

Production and Machineries

In terms of production, PT. Leuwijaya Utama Textile emphasizes on the concept of 'achieving customer satisfaction through high quality product'. To achieve high quality product, the raw materials are carefully selected and examined. This also applies to the supplier concerned, for such is crucial to ensure the continuity of supply. Serious attention is also given to quality control over each stages of the production process; the production schedule and plan are determined in precise manner; and last but not least, the improvement of employees' skill in accordance with their respective fields continuously.
